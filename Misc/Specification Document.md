## **ODI2 – Instructions for the Project Arcanum remaster**

### **Learning Objectives**
By completing this project, students will:

- Understand how narrative systems function within RPG design.
- Learn to redesign legacy mechanics for contemporary audiences.
- Practice professional documentation used in game development.
- Align story, gameplay mechanics, and worldbuilding into a unified experience.
- Develop collaborative production workflows similar to real development teams
### **Task description:**

The objective of this project is to design a **Game Design Document (GDD)** for a modern remake of _Arcanum: Of Steamworks and Magick Obscura (2001)_. The project focuses on reinterpreting a classic RPG through contemporary design principles while preserving its core identity: player freedom, narrative reactivity, and the conflict between magic and technology.

Students will demonstrate the ability to connect **narrative design, gameplay systems, worldbuilding, and player experience** into a cohesive remake vision.

The goal is not to simply retell the original story, but to design how the remake functions as a modern interactive system.

Project will be defended via oral exam.
### **Remake —** _**Game rebuilt from the ground up**_

A **remake** recreates the game **from scratch**, often using a new engine and modern design standards.
#### **What usually changes**

- Completely new graphics and assets
- New or heavily updated gameplay systems
- Reworked controls and combat
- Redesigned levels or mechanics
- New animations, AI, and sometimes new story content
#### **What may stay**

- Same characters and overall story
- Original concept or structure
### **Formatting**

- Font: Arial, size 12
- Line spacing: 1.15
- Length: **70–90 standard text pages** (1500–1800 characters including spaces per page)
- Maximum recommended length: under 100 pages
- Images allowed and encouraged (mandatory for visual sections)
### **Submission Components**

The final submission must include:
- Written GDD document
- Access to Miro board (story and system mapping)
- PPT presentation / pitch
- Clearly marked contributions of each team member
- Oral defense/presentation via Teams
### **Participation**

- Every team member must participate to minimally 18% of written output – easily reviewed by word count – any deviation **can** be defended in oral exam.
- Clearely noted roles in the project need to be written at the start of the document(not included in word count).
- Visualy communicated parts of every team member should be made as in GDD
### **AI**

Yes, but has to be noted as it is in this document.

No more than 30% of content can be created or helped to be written by AI.

Overuse and/or missuse of AI will be considered as failing the exam permanently.
### **Instructions for Teamwork**
#### **Team Roles**

Each team must assign:

**Team Coordinator (Producer Role)**

- Organizes documentation and workflow
- Maintains communication channels (Teams, Miro, Discord or equivalent)
- Communicates directly with the lecturer
- Oversees deadlines and document consistency

**Design Responsibilities (distributed by team decision):**

- Narrative Design – story and characters(short review and key changes)
- Gameplay Systems Design – everyone
- Worldbuilding & Lore
- Visual and Audio Direction
- Research & Analysis

Task distribution must be balanced. Uneven contribution lowers evaluation results.
### **GDD must include:**
#### **1: Story-wise**

- 1 new city
- 1 new companion – background story
- 1 new companion questline
- New starting story for the protagonist(short – no more than 2 cards of text)
- 1 major change in main plot
- Main plot review + proposed changes
- Worldbuild review
- Companion rework – cut the number to max 12 and reintroduce them to the game – use a method from existing similar games and elaborate why it was chosen
- Antagonist overview
#### **2. Mechanics and gameplay**

- 5 core systems – remade + elaborate why those systems
- 2 secondary systems - remade + elaborate why those systems
- Explain how this changed the game for the better
- Short review of the core gameplay loop
#### **3. Art and audio**

- Define artstyle change and elaborate why it was chosen
- Define Audio – Main theme, 3 combat themes, 5 city and special area themes, 3 relaxing music themes + 2 your choice special music uses
- Elaborate your Audio choices as a whole – 1 card of text – no more than 3
#### **4. Game Overview**

- Remake title (working title allowed)
- Short project pitch (1–2 paragraphs)
- Genre and subgenre
- Target audience
- Player fantasy (what the player experiences emotionally and mechanically)
- Core themes of the remake
#### **5. Analysis of the Original Game**

Provide a concise analysis of _Arcanum (2001)_:

- Strengths of the original game
- Weaknesses and outdated elements
- Player reception (based on reviews and historical context)
#### **6.Remake Focus**

- Clearly define:
- Key improvements
- New content added
- Content removed or redesigned
- Design reasoning behind each decision
- **Use the argumentation and examples from similar games you used as a blueprint for the remake – this is bold for a reason**

**(Argumentation is required, not opinion.)**
### **How should it look in the document**
#### **Title Page**

**Game Title:** State the name of the original game and the remastered version.  
**Authors:** Names of the students and their roles.  
**Date:** Date of document creation.  
**Document Version:** Document version identifier.

1. Content
2. Original game analysis (3-5 cards of text)
3. Your game version Overview
4. Analysis of changes, cuts and your focus – can go at the end if you wish
5. Story
6. Systems and gameplay
7. Art – visual and audio
### **How to do it**
#### **Worldbuilding**

Describe the recreated world of Arcanum:

- Setting overview (industrial fantasy world)
- Tone and atmosphere
- Cultural and technological tensions
- Major regions and locations
- Factions and social structures

Include emphasis on:

- What is preserved from the original
- What is expanded or modernized
#### **Story Summary**

- Main plot structure divided into story phases or acts
- Major plot points
#### **Narrative Changes**

- Main story adjustments
- Side quest redesigns
- Companion character expansions
- New or revised NPCs
#### **Gameplay Systems (Major Section and Focus of the GDD)**

Describe **at least five main gameplay systems** redesigned for the remake:

Examples:

- Character progression and builds
- Magic vs. Technology mechanics
- Dialogue and persuasion systems
- Combat redesign
- Reputation and faction systems

**For each system include:**

- Original implementation
- Problems or limitations
- Remake solution
- Design justification
    
This section must include argumentation, not preference statements.
#### **Gameplay Loops**

Explain the core gameplay loops created by your redesigned systems:
- Exploration loop
- Narrative decision loop
- Progression loop
- Combat or social interaction loop

Provide reasoning supported by examples from comparable RPGs.
#### **Visual Direction**

Include:
- Visual references and mood direction
- Artistic modernization approach
- Environmental design philosophy
- Comparison with contemporary RPG visuals

Visual arguments must explain _why_ changes are appropriate.
#### **Audio Direction**

- Music style and soundtrack direction
- Atmosphere goals
- References and justification for chosen audio approach
#### **Additions and New Features**

Provide a short list of:
- New gameplay features
- Quality-of-life improvements
- Modern systems added to support accessibility or replayability

If you want, you can also create a **short “student-friendly checklist version”** that teams can actually follow week-by-week while working by using this prompt in ChatGPT - give him this document as a guideline
### **Grading Criteria — Arcanum Remake GDD Project**

**Maximum Score: 80 Points**

This project evaluates both **design quality** and **professional teamwork execution**. The Game Design Document must function as real development documentation — clear, structured, and collaboratively produced.

#### **1. Coverage of Required Content (Essentials) — 20 points**

Evaluation of whether all mandatory project elements are present and sufficiently developed.

**Full points (17–20):**

- All required sections included:
    - Game overview
    - Original game analysis
    - Story redesign elements
    - Worldbuilding updates
    - New city and companion content
    - Gameplay systems (5 core + 2 secondary)
    - Gameplay loop explanation
    - Art and audio direction
    - Remake focus with argumentation
- Requirements are complete, not superficial.
- Design reasoning is clearly explained.

**Medium (10–16):**
- Most required parts included but some lack depth or justification.

**Low (0–9):**
- Missing required sections or incomplete implementation.
#### **2. Clarity & Documentation Quality — 15 points**

The document must read as **professional development documentation**, not an essay.

**Full points (13–15):**
- Clear and precise writing.
- Easy to navigate and understand.
- Concepts explained for developers, not only readers.
- Terminology used consistently.
- Arguments understandable without verbal explanation.

**Medium (7–12):**
- Generally readable but unclear explanations or inconsistent terminology.

**Low (0–6):**
- Confusing structure, unclear descriptions, or difficult to follow.
#### **3. Document Structure & Organization — 10 points**

Evaluation of formatting, hierarchy, and logical flow.

**Full points (9–10):**

- Logical order of sections.
- Proper headings and subsection hierarchy.
- Strong visual readability.
- Formatting requirements respected.
- Sections connect naturally.

**Medium (5–8):**

- Structure present but uneven or inconsistent.

**Low (0–4):**

- Disorganized or poorly structured document.
#### **4. Creative Design Effort & Remake Vision — 10 points**

Measures originality and thoughtful reinterpretation of _Arcanum_.

**Full points (9–10):**

- Clear remake identity, not a copy of the original.
- Creative but believable redesign choices.
- New content fits world tone and themes.
- Demonstrates design ambition and problem solving.

**Medium (5–8):**

- Some creative ideas but safe or underdeveloped.

**Low (0–4):**

- Minimal reinterpretation or mostly descriptive work.
#### **5. Gameplay & Narrative Integration — 15 points**

How well systems, story, and worldbuilding function together.

**Full points (13–15):**

- Gameplay mechanics reinforce narrative themes.
- Magic vs. technology conflict expressed through systems.
- Player choices meaningfully affect world and story.
- Systems support player experience coherently.

**Medium (6–12):**

- Connections exist but feel partial or inconsistent.

**Low (0–5):**

- Gameplay and narrative feel separated.
#### **6. Project Cohesion & Overall Completeness — 5 points**

Evaluation of whether the project feels like a unified game rather than disconnected parts.

**Full points (5):**

- All sections support a single design vision.
- Tone, mechanics, and narrative align.
- Document feels production-ready.

**Partial (2–4):**

- Some inconsistencies between sections.

**Low (0–1):**

- Fragmented or contradictory design.
#### **7. Team Integration & Collaboration Quality — 5 points**

Focus on how well individual contributions connect.

**Full points (5):**

- Work from different students integrates seamlessly.
- No visible “separate mini-projects.”
- Design decisions reference each other across sections.
    
**Partial (2–4):**

- Minor disconnect between sections.

**Low (0–1):**

- Clearly separated individual work with weak coordination.
#### **8. Equal Participation & Contribution Balance — 5 points**

Based on documented roles, word count participation, and oral defense.

**Full points (5):**

- All five members contribute approximately equally (minimum participation threshold respected).
- Roles clearly defined.
- Each member demonstrates understanding of the full project.
    
**Partial (2–4):**

- Slight imbalance but justified.

**Low (0–1):**

- Major imbalance or unclear contribution.
