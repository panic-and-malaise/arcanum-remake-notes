Classes are additionally given one of **4 roles** in combat:
1. **Striker**
2. **Defender**
3. **Crowd**-control
4. **Support**

Classes can **specialize in one role or cover multiple** depending on the exact build the player chooses for them.

Additionally, **multi-classing** is allowed, with a **maximum of 2 classes** for a single character, with the penalty being **slower levelling** of both classes, and an **inability to gain the highest level skills** of each class.
### Attributes

**Six** main attributes, with clear bonus effects per level:

1. **Might** (+3% Damage and Healing | +2 Fortitude per point)
2. **Constitution** (+5% Health per point | +2 Fortitude per point)
3. **Dexterity** (+3% Action Speed per point | +2 Reflex per point | +4% Stride per point)
4. **Perception** (+1 Accuracy per point | +2 Reflex per point)
5. **Intellect** (+10% Area of Effect per point | +5% Duration per point | +2 Will per point)
6. **Resolve** (-3% Hostile Effect Duration | +1 Deflection per point | +2 Will per point)

Attributes are defined at character creation and cannot be permanently altered thereafter; however, small bonuses may be gained from equipment.
### Stats

Stats define how a character can act **during combat**. They all increase by a fixed amount (3) per level, with additional levels being granted from race or subclass bonuses.

Stats are divided into:
1. **Accuracy**
2. **Penetration**
3. **Stride**
4. **Deflection**
5. **Recovery**
6. **Fortitude**
7. **Reflex**
8. **Will**
### Skills

Skills determine character proficiency in certain areas, both in and out of combat. They are divided into passive and active, with genuinely no clear delineation between what those categories even mean.

One active skill and passive skill may be increased per level-up.

**Active skills**:
1. Alchemy
2. Arcana
3. Athletics
4. Explosives
5. Mechanics
6. Sleight of Hand
7. Stealth

**Passive skills**:
1. Bluff
2. Diplomacy
3. History
4. Insight
5. Intimidate
6. Metaphysics
7. Religion
8. Streetwise
9. Survival
### Abilities and skill tree

The actions and bonuses given to characters during combat, divided into **Active** and **Passive**.

Available character abilities and their strength are determined by the character's **power level**, which increases at fixed levels (once every **two** levels for single class, or every **three for multi-class**).

Abilities can be acquired using ability points, gained at a rate of 1.5 per level (2, then 1, alternating). Some abilities additionally have other abilities as prerequisites.

### Level progression

Character level starts at **1** and maxes out at **20**. Each level grants **3 stat points** and a class-dependent amount of **additional max health**.

A character's **power level** rises on average **every 2 levels**, allowing them access to new abilities and strengthening those already obtained. An **average of 1.5 ability points** is given per level (2, then 1, alternating), which can be used to **acquire** Active and Passive **abilities**.

Additionally, one passive and one active **skill** may be upgraded at each level.
